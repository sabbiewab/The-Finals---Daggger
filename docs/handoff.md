# Handoff: resin printer ventilation + THE FINALS dagger prop

## Resin printer exhaust (Anycubic Photon M3 Max)
- Stock setup: 3" fan + hose, airflow too weak. Venting fumes outside through a window.
- Stock fan likely blows through a charcoal filter. Plan: remove the filter since the exhaust goes outside (main restriction). Seal all duct joints and the window vent.
- If still weak: add a 3" inline centrifugal fan (AC copper motor, ~200 m³/h / ~115-120 CFM, with speed controller), mounted partway down the hose toward the window. Avoid 12V axial "booster" fans. Check 75 mm vs 80 mm (3.15") fit.
- 4" option: 3"→4" increaser at the port + 4" inline fan (e.g., AC Infinity Cloudline S4).
- Watch resin temperature (keep ~20-30°C) now that cold outside air gets pulled in. Run the fan low while printing, high afterward.
- Using a 400x300 silicone mat on the enclosure floor to catch drips. Keep it clear of any intake gaps.
